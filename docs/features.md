# Features

Atlasium is in early development. This page lists what it will do and what works today.

| Feature | Status |
| --- | --- |
| Slash commands (`/atlasium version`, `/atlasium debug`, `/atlasium minimap button on` or `off`, `/atlasium minimap zoom on` or `off`, `/atlasium worldmap zoom on` or `off`, `/atlasium worldmap fog on` or `off`) | Available |
| Minimap button | Available |
| Minimap wheel zoom | Available |
| Fog clearing | Available |
| Smooth map navigation (drag and zoom) | Available |
| Map notes | Planned |
| TomTom integration | Planned |
| Questie integration | Planned |
| Party integration | Planned |

## Minimap button

A round button on the edge of the minimap. Click it to open or close the world map, and drag it
to any spot around the minimap. See [Usage](usage.md#minimap-button).

## Minimap wheel zoom

Turn the mouse wheel over the minimap to zoom it in or out, one step per notch, like the + and -
buttons. See [Usage](usage.md#minimap-wheel-zoom).

## Fog clearing

The world map shows every area of a zone, also the ones you have not explored yet. Unexplored
areas are a little darker, so you can still see where you have not been. See
[Usage](usage.md#fog-clearing).

## Smooth map navigation

Move around the world map the way you do in an online map: turn the mouse wheel to zoom in up
to 4x around the cursor, and drag with the left mouse button to move the zoomed map. Icons such
as your arrow and the quest markers keep their size. See [Usage](usage.md#map-zoom-and-drag).

## Map notes

Pin your own notes on the map, for example the spot of a rare mob, a vendor or a farming route.

## TomTom integration

If you have TomTom installed, Atlasium can set waypoints and use TomTom's arrow to guide you.
TomTom is optional: Atlasium works without it.

## Questie integration

If you have Questie installed, Atlasium can show its quests and objectives on the map.
Questie is optional: Atlasium works without it.

## Party integration

Atlasium can share waypoints, pings and notes with party members who also have Atlasium. See
[Party integration](party.md).
